About Kerehosahalli (ಕೆರೆಹೊಸಹಳ್ಳಿ)

Village Highlights & Key Information

Kerehosahalli is a vibrant and peaceful village located in the Ajjampura / Tarikere Taluk region of Chikkamagaluru district, Karnataka (Postal PIN Code: 577228).

Known for its rich spiritual tradition, close-knit community life, and agricultural heritage, Kerehosahalli is blessed with divine grace from local deities. The village comes alive during annual temple festivals and cultural gatherings where families from across the state gather.

📌 Quick Details

  • PIN Code: 577228
  • Taluk: Ajjampura / Tarikere
  • District: Chikkamagaluru, Karnataka
  • Main Agriculture: Arecanut, Coconut, Paddy, Ragi
  • Nearest Rly Station: Ajjampura / Birur Jn
